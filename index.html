Running roadmap

Build an aerobic base first; introduce speed work only when continuous running feels comfortable.

WEEKS 1–2

Establish

15–25 minutes easy run/walk, up to twice per week.

WEEKS 3–4

Extend

20–30 minutes continuous easy running, if comfortable.

WEEKS 5–6

Build

Work toward a relaxed 3–5 km. Add short strides only if recovered.

WEEKS 7–8

Sharpen

Maintain easy runs; consider controlled intervals after establishing a base.

Training rules

Warm up dynamically.
Begin with 3–5 min light cardio, joint movements and lighter sets. Save longer static stretches for after training.
Progress carefully.
Rest ~2 min on demanding compound lifts and 60–90 sec on isolation sets. Prioritize controlled technique over load.
Recover seriously.
Keep Wednesday and Sunday free of structured workouts. Aim for adequate sleep, food and hydration.
Adjust to your baseline.
Running progression and exercise loads are illustrative. Reduce volume if new, sore or fatigued. Stop if sharp pain occurs.
